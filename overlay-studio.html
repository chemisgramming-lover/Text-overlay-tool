Welcome to Overlayer

Stack text and pictures on top of a photo, then save the result. Here's the flow:

  1. 1Photo tab — add your background photo first.
  2. 2Text or Overlay tab — add something to place on top of it.
  3. 3Drag the new layer to move it, pull the corner dot ⤡ to resize, and the top dot ↻ to rotate.
  4. 4Use the layer's panel to adjust color, outline, shadow, or blending so it stands out.
  5. 5Tap Save photo up top when you're happy with it.

Tip: only things you add via Text/Overlay are draggable — the background photo itself stays fixed.
